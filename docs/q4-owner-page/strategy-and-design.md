# Enchanted Havens owner page strategy and design

Draft for Q4 2026 · October 25, 2026

Enchanted Havens should present a clear full-service management offer for owners of distinctive Pacific Northwest vacation homes. The page should earn trust through named properties and dated results, show the marketing audience EH has already built, explain how the home will be cared for, and make starting a conversation easy.

The recommended position is **exceptional homes, documented performance, and personal care**. Revenue evidence attracts attention; the management scope and people behind it give an owner confidence to inquire. The primary action is **Tell us about your home**, opening a short form on the same page, with call and text alternatives.

This is a fresh proposal. The earlier owner-growth packages, fees, targets, and operating model are not inputs to this plan. These documents cover content and design; they do not change the live website.

## Audience and offer

Prioritize owners of established or prospective vacation homes in EH's existing operating areas: the Olympic Peninsula around Port Angeles, Hood Canal around Belfair, and Whidbey Island around Oak Harbor. Use Pacific Northwest as the brand's regional context while being specific about service availability.

The page needs to answer three different owner situations without requiring a visitor to select a funnel:

- An owner with another manager wants evidence, better communication, and a practical transition.
- A self-managing owner wants time back while retaining visibility into the home and its performance.
- A new vacation-home owner wants an honest assessment of readiness, likely demand, and the work involved.

Sell one main offer: full-service vacation rental management. Explain the service in terms of responsibilities, expected communication, and care. Do not introduce tiers or fees until the commercial model is confirmed.

## Service strengths to develop

**A recognizable hospitality brand.** Each home has a distinct name, photography, and guest experience within a coherent collection. Owners can see how EH would present their property by exploring real examples.

**Marketing with an existing audience.** Instagram and the direct-booking website are observable assets. Show actual creative work and its reach. Attribute bookings or revenue to social only when the tracking supports that connection.

**Revenue decisions grounded in the property.** Explain seasonal pricing, calendar availability, minimum stays, presentation, and amenities in plain language. A property assessment should use relevant comparable homes and realistic assumptions. The page should distinguish gross revenue from owner proceeds.

**Care that protects the guest experience and the home.** Describe turnover checks, maintenance coordination, and escalation responsibilities. One authentic operational photo and a concise example of an inspection process would be stronger than a generic shield icon. Confirm the actual scope and emergency coverage before turning these into commitments.

**A clear owner relationship.** Identify the person or team responsible for the account, explain financial reporting and spending approvals, and describe how owner stays are arranged. These are useful differentiators only when the operational details are specific.

**Experience across different PNW settings.** Lakefront, forest, canal, and island homes provide substantive case material. The page can demonstrate local knowledge without claiming coverage throughout every part of the PNW.

The current website's August 2026 snapshot reports a 4.92 Airbnb rating across 752 guest reviews. This may support the hospitality story after refresh, or with its date attached. It must remain clearly identified as guest feedback, not owner satisfaction.

The first inquiry action must be visible before the owner reads the full page. Repeat it after the evidence and at the end. Offer “Explore our results” as a quieter anchor near the hero.

## Visual direction

Use the existing EH design language: Cormorant Garamond display type, Manrope body type, forest green `#173c33`, deep forest `#071e19`, ivory `#f3eee3`, paper `#faf7f0`, and restrained brass `#805a27` or `#d4b47d` accents. Keep the current logo and header proportions.

The hero should feel like an EH hospitality page: a home in its landscape, warm light, generous spacing, and a large serif headline. Use an existing property image; a Sea-Renity exterior is suitable for the first concept. Do not use stock business handshakes or an invented house.

Alternate photography and editorial text with calm ivory sections. Present the ranking evidence as a precise table or aligned rows, not a collection of colorful badges. Use thin rules, square or subtly rounded corners, and one primary button treatment. Large numbers must always have nearby geographic and metric labels.

Add **For Owners** to the existing navigation. The guest booking navigation can remain, but the primary page action should lead to owner inquiries. The existing contact page asks about a stay; it is not a suitable substitute for the owner form.

On mobile, stack the hero content and evidence; retain market names, denominators, and dates. Use a compact bottom inquiry action after the hero leaves view, with call and text reachable without obscuring content. Resolve overlap with guest chat or booking controls. Keep inputs at least 16px, tap targets around 44px, and focus states visible. Avoid autoplay video and heavy Instagram embeds on initial load; use owned stills with links to the originals.

## Inquiry experience

The inquiry form requires **Name**, **Email**, **Property address** (street, city, state, ZIP), **Bedrooms**, and **Bathrooms** so the team can research the home before following up. Accept 0 bedrooms for studios and half bathrooms. Group the property details together, with bedroom and bathroom inputs side by side. Offer optional checkboxes for **Oceanfront**, **Lakefront**, **Scenic view**, **Hot tub**, **Sauna**, and **Pet-friendly**. Keep **Phone** and **Anything you would like us to know?** optional; the note can include an existing listing URL or other features.

Use the same CTA wording throughout: **Tell us about your home**. The form submit label is **Send my inquiry**. Explain that the next step is a conversation about the property and the owner's goals. Do not require a calendar booking, account, document upload, or revenue disclosure to submit.

For the draft, the existing public phone is `(360) 230-8143` and email is `stays@enchantedhavens.com`. Before launch, designate an owner-inquiry recipient and confirm the phone supports both calls and texts. Promise a reply time only after staffing is agreed.

Design success, error, and retry states. Retain entered content after an error. Confirm receipt only after the inquiry has been accepted. Route owner submissions separately from guest requests, retain their source, and prevent duplicate delivery on retries. Do not automatically subscribe an owner to marketing.

## SEO and AI search plan

Use **/vacation-rental-management** as the descriptive canonical page, with a permanent redirect from the legacy **/management** path at launch. The legacy path currently returns a 404. Link the owner page from the header, footer, story page, and appropriate property pages.

Proposed title: **PNW Vacation Rental Management | Enchanted Havens**.

Proposed description: **Full-service vacation rental management in the Pacific Northwest. Explore Enchanted Havens property results, marketing reach, and care for your home.**

Target owner intent: Pacific Northwest vacation rental management, Olympic Peninsula vacation rental management, Port Angeles Airbnb management, Hood Canal vacation rental management, and Whidbey Island vacation rental management. These are topic targets, not measured search-volume findings. The main page should explain the specific service footprint in natural prose.

Original property evidence is the main content advantage. Publish the results and method as readable HTML, with named homes, source links, metric definitions, and a refresh date. Add short substantive answers to real owner questions. Do not make important information accessible only in screenshots, PDFs, videos, or a form-gated download.

Keep consistent business identity, contact details, and service areas. Extend the existing Organization identity with an appropriate owner contact and Service description; use BreadcrumbList where applicable. Structured data must match visible content. Guest ratings must not be repurposed as an aggregate rating for management services.

Google's current guidance emphasizes original useful content, crawlability, indexing, snippet eligibility, and inclusion in Search generative AI features through Search Console. There is no special AI schema requirement, and `llms.txt` does not improve Google visibility. Useful owner FAQs still belong on the page, but FAQ rich results have been discontinued. [Google AI guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) · [Google documentation updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates).

For ChatGPT search, review access for OAI-SearchBot in robots and hosting controls. This is separate from GPTBot training permissions. Maintain normal security controls while allowing legitimate search crawling. [OpenAI crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots).

Create dedicated market pages later only when each has original local evidence, actual service coverage, and useful operating knowledge. Do not generate interchangeable town pages. A practical expansion is one Olympic Peninsula page, one Hood Canal page, and one Whidbey Island page supported by distinct case studies.

## Measurement and publication sequence

Measure qualified owner conversations and eventual signed management agreements. Supporting measures are owner-intent search impressions and clicks, engaged owner-page visits, accepted form submissions, call/text clicks, completed consultations, proposals, and signed homes. A click is not a call; a form submission is not a qualified opportunity.

Use Search Console's web and generative AI reporting where available, plus analytics referrers and a lightweight optional attribution question during follow-up. AI referrals are not fully observable. Establish a baseline before setting targets; the scrapped plan's numerical targets do not carry forward.

The remaining design work is a review of this copy and the proposed visual composition, then a complete desktop and mobile treatment including form states. Implementation begins only in a later phase.

Before publication, close the material content gaps: confirm the management responsibilities and owner terms; identify the inquiry recipient and phone route; refresh the AirDNA evidence and verify its precise reporting window; confirm acceptable public use of the selected data; obtain Instagram Insights if using organic or local-audience claims; and add authentic team information. An owner testimonial is desirable, but the page can launch without one.
