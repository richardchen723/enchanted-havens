AirDNA defines Revenue LTM as estimated listing revenue over the last twelve months, including cleaning fees. Its model is not an audited owner income statement. Revenue Potential estimates full-time availability and must not replace Revenue in a performance claim. [AirDNA metric definitions](https://enterprise-help.airdna.co/en/articles/8185673-property-performance-data).

The viewed ranking screen did not expose exact start and end months. Describe the data as AirDNA trailing-twelve-month estimates viewed October 25, 2026; do not call it calendar-year 2026 performance. Capture the reporting window and reproducible filter state with the final publication evidence. AirDNA's overview active-listing count is a different measure from the ranking view's “Listings shown” denominator.

Market labels are essential: Port Angeles is not the entire Olympic Peninsula, Oak Harbor is not all of Whidbey Island, and Belfair is not all of Hood Canal. The six properties span different sizes and settings; this broad ranking does not establish like-for-like uplift from EH management.
