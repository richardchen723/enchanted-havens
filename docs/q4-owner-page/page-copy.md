## Owner questions

**What does full-service management include?**

Our proposed scope covers property presentation, pricing and calendar management, marketing, guest communication, turnover coordination, and owner communication. The management proposal sets out the responsibilities and any separately charged services for your home.

**How much could my property earn?**

That depends on the home, location, amenities, available dates, and seasonal demand. We review relevant comparisons and explain the assumptions behind an assessment. Results from other EH homes provide context; they are not a guarantee of your property's revenue.

**What are your management fees?**

We discuss fees alongside the service scope and the needs of your property. Your proposal should make clear what is included and which expenses are separate before you decide to proceed.

**Can I stay in my own home?**

Tell us how you would like to use your home. We will explain how personal stays, booking availability, and any related costs are handled under the management agreement.

**Can you help if I already have a manager?**

We can discuss a transition plan, including existing reservations, listing arrangements, access, and the responsibilities under your current agreement. Timing depends on those details.

**Do you manage homes throughout the Pacific Northwest?**

Our current collection includes homes around Port Angeles on the Olympic Peninsula, Belfair on Hood Canal, and Oak Harbor on Whidbey Island. Share your property's location so we can confirm availability in your area.

**Does my home need to be rental-ready?**

You can contact us while you are still planning. We can discuss the home's current condition, presentation, and the steps involved in preparing it for guests. The scope of any setup work will be agreed separately.
