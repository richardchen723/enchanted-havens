Of **116 reviewed property claims**, 98 were supported, 2 incorrect and 16 unverified: **84.48% supported**. Verified-only accuracy is 98/100, but that excludes unsupported claims and must not be presented alone. The two confirmed errors were Blue Haven’s capacity (model: two to four; confirmed: six) and pet-fee basis (model: per pet; confirmed: flat USD 100 per stay). Unsupported claims included nightly prices, Wi-Fi performance, kayak type and access/equipment details. No identifiable retired-property recommendation was observed.

Qualification behavior was appropriate in 8 of 11 assessed responses (72.73%); other responses were not graded for that metric. This checks whether the answer distinguishes unverified requirements and asks for confirmation. It does not validate competitor inventory or legal statements. Separate regional errors included a ferry-route name, the direction of Lake Sutherland from Port Angeles, and a false calendar-window explanation.

The two failed samples were workspace-proof sample 1 (a fulfillment error) and seattle-weekend sample 1 (the conversation reset to an unsent composer after an extended search). Neither is counted as a negative recommendation. The remaining samples were completed independently; no regeneration was used to seek better rankings. Raw evidence and reviewed annotations are retained locally in `output/ai-visibility/2026-10-27/`. The redacted aggregate is `baseline-2026-10-27.json`.
